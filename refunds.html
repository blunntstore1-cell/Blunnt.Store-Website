Cancellations

To request a cancellation, contact us as soon as possible after placing the order. We can cancel an order only before it has been dispatched. If approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos or video of the issue. We will review the request and, where applicable, arrange a replacement, return or refund.

Returns

Items must be unused, unwashed, unworn and returned with original tags and packaging. Return eligibility is assessed after we receive the request and verify the item condition.

Refund timing

Once a refund is approved, it is initiated to the original payment method. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on Razorpay, your bank or payment provider.
