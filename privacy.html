This policy explains how BLUNNT collects and uses information when you visit our website or place an order.

Information we collect

We collect the information you provide during checkout, including your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and order details. Payment processing is handled by Razorpay and Shopify. BLUNNT does not store your complete card, UPI or bank-payment details.

How we use it

We use your information to process and deliver orders, send order and shipping updates, provide customer support, prevent fraud, and improve our store.

Who receives it

We share only the necessary information with Shopify, Razorpay, shipping and fulfilment partners, and service providers needed to operate the store. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and choices

We retain order information as required for fulfilment, tax, accounting, fraud prevention and legal obligations. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information where applicable.
